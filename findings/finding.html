A closer look: does full size show more?

I hoped to find a spot where reading at full size shows something the team's small online preview hides. Honestly, I didn't. The AI's map is soft by nature, so shrinking it loses very little: below, the same row of marks looks almost the same both ways. (The team also keeps full-size files; they're just big to download.)

What full size does add is the papyrus underneath. With the gold laid on the real X-ray, you can see how the marks sit on the woven fibres of the sheet, and zoom right in without anything turning into blocks.
