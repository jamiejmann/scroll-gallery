What does the gold mean?

The ink on these scrolls is made of soot, just like the burnt papyrus, so it's nearly invisible in X-rays. An AI that learned from known letters paints gold wherever it thinks ink is. Brighter gold means it's more confident. It's a very good guess, not a reading.

For the curious: how this picture was made

This is one segment: a stretch of papyrus that the Vesuvius Challenge team traced through a 3D X-ray scan and flattened into a picture. The ink map is the team's Timesformer ink model for this scroll, published by the team in the open data and used here with credit. We did not re-run the model. The scan view is the X-ray surface (the middle layers), sometimes shown at a slightly lower resolution so the site stays quick to load.

Size: 7,331 × 8,192 pixels (about 58 × 65 mm).
