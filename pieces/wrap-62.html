What does the gold mean?

The ink on these scrolls is made of soot, just like the burnt papyrus, so it's nearly invisible in X-rays. An AI that learned from known letters paints gold wherever it thinks ink is. Brighter gold means it's more confident. It's a very good guess, not a reading.

For the curious: how this picture was made

This is one segment: a stretch of papyrus that the Vesuvius Challenge team traced through a 3D X-ray scan (8.64 micrometres per pixel, taken at the ESRF in Grenoble) and flattened into a picture. The ink map was made with Reader V3 by Domenico Russo, running on an ordinary processor (CPU) with the free reader at github.com/jamiejmann/vesuvius-cpu-reader. The scan view is the average of the five middle layers.

Size: 8,760 × 4,760 pixels (about 76 × 41 mm). Reading time: 8.6 minutes on 6 CPU threads. The team's picture is their own Reader V3 result, shared as a smaller preview (1/8 size).
